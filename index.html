How Recommendations Work

How are songs selected?

Songs are selected based on their overall mood, energy, atmosphere, and listening experience.

Why use mood-based recommendations?

Mood categories make it easier to discover music based on how you feel or how you want to feel.

Are the recommendations personalized?

The recommendations are curated suggestions rather than predictions. Music preferences can vary from person to person.

About Recommendations

This platform uses curated playlists to match your mood with the perfect songs for any occasion. Songs are picked based on your emotional state and preferences. Rather than choosing songs from lyrics, the focus is on the overall vibe and atmosphere of each track.

Music is personal, so recommendations are meant to inspire rather than perfectly predict your preferences.
